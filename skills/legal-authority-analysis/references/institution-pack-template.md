# Institution pack template

Copy this into the project (for example `packs/ecthr.md`) and fill it from sources checked in a session. Write `UNVERIFIED` in any section not checked; a blank reads as unfinished, `UNVERIFIED` reads as checked and not confirmed.

```markdown
---
institution: [NAME]
last_checked: [YYYY-MM-DD or "never"]
checked_by: [NAME or "none"]
---

# [Institution]

## Mandate and structure
Formations (chamber, grand chamber, committee, panel), their relationship, and which decisions are final.

## Authoritative sources
Official repository and database; official mirrors; what to do when the official site blocks access.

## Document types and identity traps
Judgment, decision, advisory opinion, views, order, report. Stages (admissibility, merits, interpretation, revision, reparations). Versions (provisional, final, corrected, redacted). The confusions that recur.

## Citation form
The form the venue or project style requires, with one checked example per document type.

## Foundational texts
Constituent instrument, rules of procedure, practice directions; which version applies at which date.

## Not authority
Press releases, summaries, factsheets, NGO reports, commentaries: useful leads, not evidence of what the body held.

## Interpretation notes
Distinctive interpretive approaches and standards of review, each with a checked source.
```
