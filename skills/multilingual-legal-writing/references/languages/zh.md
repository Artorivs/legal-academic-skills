# Chinese

Four legal systems write in Chinese and they are not interchangeable. Fix the system first, then the script (Simplified or Traditional), then the terminology. Script does not determine system: Simplified Chinese can be written about Hong Kong law, and Traditional about PRC law.

## Legal systems

- **PRC (mainland):** civil-law tradition with socialist-law features. Chinese is the only official language of legislation.
- **Hong Kong SAR:** common law. Chinese and English texts of legislation have equal status (Interpretation and General Clauses Ordinance (Cap 1) s 10B, as described by the [Department of Justice](https://www.doj.gov.hk/en/about/orgchart_ldd_drafting_chi_eng.html)). Many judgments are in English only; terminology follows the bilingual legislation, not PRC usage.
- **Macao SAR:** civil law derived from Portuguese law; Chinese and Portuguese are official. UNVERIFIED beyond this line.
- **Taiwan:** civil-law tradition. Since 4 January 2022, under the Constitutional Court Procedure Act (憲法訴訟法), the Constitutional Court issues judgments (判決) and rulings (裁定) rather than the earlier interpretations (解釋) ([source](https://www.legis-pedia.com/article/other/938)). Cite earlier 釋字 and later 憲判字 decisions in their own forms; check the form on the court's site.

## PRC source hierarchy

Keep these categories distinct; a common error is to treat everything below 法律 as one class.

1. 宪法
2. 法律 (National People's Congress and its Standing Committee)
3. 行政法规 (State Council)
4. 地方性法规, 自治条例和单行条例 (local people's congresses)
5. 规章: 部门规章 (ministries and commissions) and 地方政府规章 (local governments). These are rules with a defined legal status; they are not 规范性文件.
6. 规范性文件: other normative documents issued by administrative bodies, below 规章.
7. 司法解释 (Supreme People's Court and Supreme People's Procuratorate): binding interpretations on the application of law; distinct from legislation and from cases.
8. Cases: 指导性案例 (guiding cases) and, since the 人民法院案例库 opened on 27 February 2024, 参考案例 (reference cases) collected with guiding cases in one database ([SPC announcement](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/426222.html)). Describe their effect on later courts from the current SPC rules, not from memory.

Party documents and policy statements are relevant evidence of policy but are not sources of law in the hierarchy above; say which you are relying on.

## Primary-source databases

- PRC: 国家法律法规数据库 (flk.npc.gov.cn); 人民法院案例库; 中国裁判文书网 (coverage and access have changed over time; record what was available when searched); 北大法宝 (subscription).
- Hong Kong: Hong Kong e-Legislation (elegislation.gov.hk); the Judiciary's Legal Reference System; HKLII.
- Taiwan: 全國法規資料庫 (law.moj.gov.tw); 司法院法學資料檢索系統; the Constitutional Court's site.

## Scholarship databases

CNKI (中国知网) for mainland journals; 月旦知識庫 and the Airiti Library (華藝) for Taiwan; HKU Scholars Hub and HKLII's journal collection for Hong Kong. Coverage of each is partial; record which were searched.

## Citation conventions

- PRC: many journals follow 《法学引注手册》 or their own house rules; check the target journal.
- Hong Kong: English-language venues commonly use OSCOLA-style or journal-specific rules; neutral citations such as [year] HKCFA n.
- Taiwan: journal-specific; check the target journal.

## Register

Scholarly Chinese is not the register of 司法解释, government reports, or court judgments. Avoid importing bureaucratic or policy language (for example 抓手, 赋能, 顶层设计, 高质量发展 used as analysis) unless the text is analysing that language. Keep one term per concept throughout (for example 个人信息 or 个人数据, not both).

## Drift-prone terms

| Term | Pitfall | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 合同 / 合約 / 契約 | Usage differs between PRC, Hong Kong, and Taiwan | Follow the system's legislation |
| 判例 | Implies binding precedent | Avoid for PRC cases; use 案例, 指导性案例, or 参考案例 |
| 司法审查 | Not equivalent to common-law judicial review; PRC review of administrative acts is 行政诉讼 | Explain the mechanism rather than equating |
| 法治 / rule of law | Contested equivalence; 依法治国 is not "rule of law" in the liberal sense | Translate with a note where the argument depends on it |
| 解释 | Statutory interpretation by the NPCSC (立法解释), judicial interpretation (司法解释), and Taiwan's former 大法官解釋 are different instruments | Name the instrument |
| 基本法 | Hong Kong and Macao Basic Laws; also the German *Grundgesetz* in translation | Specify |

## Formulaic prose to avoid

首先……其次……最后 used as a template for every paragraph; 综上所述 opening an empty conclusion; 值得注意的是 and 需要指出的是 as filler; 在……背景下 openings; strings of four-character set phrases that add no content; 应当 standing in for an argument.
