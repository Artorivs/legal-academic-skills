# Language pack template

Copy to `<language-code>.md` (for example `de.md`, `es.md`, `pt.md`, `ar.md`). Fill each section from sources checked in a session and record the date. Write `UNVERIFIED` in a section you have not checked; never leave it blank.

```markdown
---
language: [NAME, CODE]
last_checked: [YYYY-MM-DD or "never"]
checked_by: [NAME or "none"]
---

# [Language]

## Legal systems that use this language
Each jurisdiction or international body separately. Note official status and whether other languages share authenticity.

## Source hierarchy, per legal system
Types of legal source in order of authority, with the local names.

## Primary-source databases
Official legislation and case-law repositories, per system.

## Scholarship databases
Where legal scholarship in this language is indexed.

## Citation conventions
The standard guide or the absence of one; what journals expect.

## Register
How legal scholarship in this language differs from legislative and judicial register.

## False friends and drift-prone terms
Term, apparent equivalent, why it misleads, better rendering.

## Formulaic prose to avoid
Stock phrases that mark machine-generated or padded text in this language.
```
