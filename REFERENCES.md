# Design references

- Matt Pocock, `mattpocock/skills` (GitHub): https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main — used as a design reference for small, composable task-specific skills and writing workflows.
- Agent Skills specification: https://agentskills.io/specification — reference for the directory-per-skill architecture and `SKILL.md` YAML frontmatter.
- OpenAI documentation, Skills: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/tools-skills — reference for OpenAI's support for the open Agent Skills format in supported agent environments.

## Argument model and language rubric (`argument-and-language-check`)

- University of Pittsburgh, Department of Communication, "Argument: Claims, Reasons, Evidence": https://www.comm.pitt.edu/argument-claims-reasons-evidence — the claim–reason–evidence model, the challenge and "because" tests for reasons, and the types of evidence and their tests. Read 16 November 2026.
- Bradley Dowden, *Logical Reasoning*, ch 2 "Claims, Issues, and Arguments" (pp 22–95 extract): https://www.sfu.ca/~swartz/logical_reasoning/02_dowden_pp_022-095.pdf — claims and issues, indicator and discount words, standard form, implicit premises (charity) and unstated conclusions (fidelity), deductive validity and inductive strength, and arguments distinguished from explanations and descriptions. Read through a fetching tool on 16 November 2026; pages after about p 82 were not seen.
- Stephen Toulmin, *The Uses of Argument* (Cambridge University Press 1958) — the warrant, backing, qualifier and rebuttal layout; cited for the general model, not for any page.
- IELTS Writing Task 2 band descriptors (public version), British Council, IDP: IELTS Australia and Cambridge English — the four-criterion structure (Task Response; Coherence and Cohesion; Lexical Resource; Grammatical Range and Accuracy) on which the language rubric is modelled. The kit's descriptors are its own wording, and its bands are not IELTS scores. Copy read 16 November 2026: https://www.dei.unict.it/sites/default/files/documenti_sito/Writing-Band-descriptors-Task-2.pdf
- Stephanie Link, *Scientific Writing for Publication*, Oklahoma State University Open Library, section 2 "For Research Argument: Claim Structure and Organization": https://open.library.okstate.edu/writingforpublication/part/section-2-for-research-argument-claim-structure-and-organization/ — suggested by the maintainer. The section page could not be opened on 16 November 2026; only the chapter "Anatomy of a Research Article" was read, and nothing in the kit relies on the rest of the section.
