## Instructions

- One row per material proposition; split compound claims.
- Never invent citations or pinpoints to complete a row.
- Use “Not yet assessed” when the source text has not been inspected.
- Record the version/date of legal sources where amendments or later treatment may matter.
- Keep analysis separate from what the source expressly says.
